karvi v0.32.1 build and qualification result #

Result #

The build, with the native scrapligo-v1 transport compiled in beside the external system transport, is qualified as an engineering candidate. v0.32.1 (2026-10-18) is a patch release on 0.32.0 for the one-letter command words and karvi-audit's wider DEVICE column: c, r, l, and s are aliases of command, run, login, and stream, and DEVICE is 30 cells, so the extended hostnames of switches, routers, and servers show whole. Every counter is 0.32.0's, and the first submission after the upgrade drains a 0.32.0 daemon. The changes are in CHANGELOG.md. It is not a production claim and not a real-device qualification: the native transport is proven against the fake device built from the tree, its IOS XE and Linux personas.

Operational upgrade sequence #

A daemon serves a client only when its daemon IPC schema and its build, the Go build ID of its executable, equal the client's. An operator's own daemon, the one the client launches (daemon.launch = "client", the default), needs nothing run after an upgrade: the next submission drains a daemon of another build, an idle one exiting and replaced in the same invocation, a busy one finishing its jobs and exiting after the last while each submission is refused daemon_draining (exit 112), the daemon's build over the client's. To end it rather than wait:

karvi daemon status
karvi daemon stop --grace

--after=DURATION drains and waits up to the limit, then forces; --force stops at once, and a forced stop accounts every unfinished unit as incomplete_shutdown. An operator's idle daemon stops itself after ten minutes (daemon.shutdown-idle-timer). A service account's daemon under the template karvi-daemon@.service is never drained by a client; after an upgrade the administrator restarts its unit:

sudo systemctl restart karvi-daemon@karvi.service

Every release is installed as new, from its package or its bundle (sudo ./scripts/install.sh); no source patch is shipped.

Remaining boundary #

What karvi does not do yet is ROADMAP.md, none of which this release claims. Cisco IOS XE laboratory qualification on real devices was not performed and follows on this executable (docs/DEVICE-QUALIFICATION-RUNBOOK.md).
